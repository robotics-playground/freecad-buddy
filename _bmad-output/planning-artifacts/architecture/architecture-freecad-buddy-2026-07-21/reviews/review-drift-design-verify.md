# Drift Review — Spine vs Design Subset

**Verdict: 2 CRITICAL, 4 HIGH, 3 MEDIUM, 2 LOW. The design subset is broadly faithful to the 23 ADs;
the load-bearing defects are one systematic render-budget contradiction against AD-10 and one stale
AD-21 judge-input list in the harness doc.**

Method note: every finding quotes both sides verbatim. Where the spine and a design doc are both
internally consistent but disagree with each other, the spine wins by the precedence rule
(`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` is authoritative). `.memlog.md` was consulted only to date the AD-10 and
AD-21 rulings; it is evidence of intent, not an authority.
