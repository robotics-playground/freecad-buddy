# Drift Review — `docs/intent.md` vs `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md`

- **Reviewed:** `docs/intent.md` (341 lines) against `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture/architecture-freecad-buddy-2026-07-21/ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` (339 lines, 23 ADs).
- **Method:** line-by-line coverage walk of every Guiding Principle, Reuse-Map row, Validation-First principle, Phase 0–5 exit criterion, Skills-Track SMART criterion, Risk row, and Out-of-Scope row. Then contradiction, silent-weakening, orphan-AD, and scope-creep passes.
- **Verdict:** coverage of the *verification core* is strong. Drift concentrates in three places: (1) **non-geometric exit criteria** (latency, wall-clock, soak, memory) have no home anywhere in the spine; (2) **two ADs quietly move scope** — AD-21 pulls Phase-5 TechDraw into Phase-3 done-ness, and AD-22/AD-23 promote one Phase-5 bullet to full AD status while six sibling bullets get nothing; (3) **the tiered n=10→n=30 eval rule** that intent states twice is contradicted by AD-14 without an amendment note.
